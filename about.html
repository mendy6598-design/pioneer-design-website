About

Interior design — from inspiration to transformation.

Spaces with a point of view

The Pioneer Design is an interior design studio creating residential and commercial spaces that feel as good as they look. We believe a room should tell you something about the people who use it — their rituals, their tastes, the way they like to live.

From striking focal points to subtle textures, every design element plays a part in that story. We balance proportion, material and light to build interiors that are calm, warm and quietly confident.

Working closely with each client from the first conversation to the final installation, we keep the process personal, considered and clear.

— The Pioneer Design
